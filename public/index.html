The position stays open. The broker doesn't.

You hold TSLA at a broker that closes at 8 PM ET. A policy headline lands on Saturday. You cannot reduce the position until Monday, and the gap prints against you.

Sell every Friday

Flatten before the weekend and you forfeit the position you wanted to hold.

Buy puts

Options need approval, cost premium, and most retail brokers gate them.

Accept the gap

Do nothing and wear whatever the market decides while you were asleep.

Tesrune adds a fourth choice: a temporary, same-name hedge with a fixed removal time.

One event. One decision. One clock.

01 Book

Paste the holdings you cannot trade overnight. Confirm the parse before anything is stored.

02 Event

During dark hours Tesrune reads Bitget MCP news, SEC 8-K filings, and perp price moves.

03 Hedge

Qwen judges materiality and direction. The mandate sizes and stamps it. You confirm.

04 Unwind

The scheduler closes the hedge at 09:29 ET and verifies the position is flat.

Qwen interprets. Hard code controls execution.

The model reads the news and forms an opinion. It never touches the exchange. These five rules decide whether an order is even allowed to exist.

01
Dark hours only No hedge proposal while your broker is open. Sell there instead.
02
Hedge only The position can be reduced, never flipped net short.
03
Delta capped A request for 150 against 100 held is clipped to 100 and logged.
04
One hedge per name A duplicate short on a name already hedged is declined.
05
Flat by the bell Every fill carries a pre-authorized unwind at 09:29 ET.
